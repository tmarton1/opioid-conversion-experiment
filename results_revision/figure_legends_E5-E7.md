## E5. Reference standards compared, by agent (`E5_reference_standards_by_agent.png`)

Oral morphine equivalent factor of each study agent under each published standard, relative to the Nielsen median (log scale), for the 14 of 19 study agents other than oral morphine that at least one alternative standard covers. Grey bars: Nielsen interquartile range; shading: tight (0.85–1.15×) and broad (0.6–1.7×) bands. 10 of 14 agents have at least one standard outside the tight band (8 excluding the superseded CDC 2016 factors, open circles); outside the broad band: Tramadol (oral) (CDC 2016 only). Nielsen is the lone outlier (all current alternatives agree with each other and differ from Nielsen) for Codeine (oral), Hydrocodone (oral), Fentanyl (patch). No alternative standard: Buprenorphine (IM), Buprenorphine (IV), Fentanyl (SC), Methadone (IV), Nalbuphine (parenteral). Model-independent.

## E6. Agreement between models (`E6_model_agreement_spread.png`)

One point per conversion (naive condition, Nielsen score, 13 models). x: within-model spread (median over models of the SD of the log ratio); y: between-model spread (SD of the 13 model medians), both plotted as exp(SD) on log axes; dashed line: identity. Open diamonds: the spread between the current standards for the same conversion (max/min fold), joined to the model point by a grey line. Random-dose pairs: between > within for 48 of 49; between-model spread exceeds the between-standard range for 36 of 40 pairs with an alternative standard (medians: within 1.41×, between models 1.92×, between standards 1.18×). Text vignettes: 20 of 20 and 18 of 19 (medians 1.17×, 1.65×, 1.2×). The vignette within-model spread pools each scenario's four wordings. Comparing a range (standards) with an SD (models) favors the standards.

## E7. Worked scoring examples (`E7_scoring_examples.png`)

For four conversions to oral morphine, the fold error each score assigns to every possible answer (curves; x = answer relative to the Nielsen expected dose, log scale) and the actual naive random-dose responses of the 13 models (histograms). Markers: the expected dose under each current standard; shading: tight and broad bands on the assigned error. Under the all-standards and all-except-Nielsen scores, an answer matching any standard in the set scores 1×; otherwise its error is the fold distance to the nearest. Responses within the tight band: oral hydrocodone → oral morphine (n = 554): Nielsen 3%, all standards 51%, all except Nielsen 48%; IV hydromorphone → oral morphine (n = 335): Nielsen 38%, all standards 40%, all except Nielsen 1%; oral oxycodone → oral morphine (n = 433): Nielsen 85%, all standards 86%, all except Nielsen 86%; oral codeine → oral morphine (n = 555): Nielsen 46%, all standards 78%, all except Nielsen 32%. Unusable answers count as outside every band.
